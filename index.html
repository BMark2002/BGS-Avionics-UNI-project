AEROSPACE-GRADE CONSTRUCTION

Machined From Solid Aluminum. Built to Outlast Thousands of Flight Hours.

Forget hollow plastic toys that flex under pressure. Every BGS Avionics faceplate is precision-milled from 3mm solid aerospace-grade aluminum and finished with a durable, glare-resistant powder coat matched to real commercial flight deck specifications.

Behind the panel, industrial-grade locking toggle switches, magnetic push-pull rotary encoders, and heavy-duty hall-effect sensors deliver the exact mechanical resistance, detent click, and tactile weight pilots expect on the flight deck.

NIGHT OPERATIONS & ILLUMINATION

Authentic Dual-Zone Backlighting for True Night Immersion.

Flying a night approach into a busy hub demands crystal-clear instrumentation without eye strain. Our custom light-guide panels distribute warm amber and crisp aviation white illumination evenly across every laser-etched legend and annunciator button.

Built to strict 1:1 commercial scale, every knob, guard, and display sits exactly where your muscle memory expects it—allowing you to adjust heading, altitude, and vertical speed instinctively without ever taking your eyes off the horizon.

ZERO-LATENCY ARCHITECTURE

Single-Cable USB-C Simplicity. Native Simulator Integration.

Spending hours wrestling with third-party script editors and broken configuration files kills the joy of flight simulation. Every BGS Avionics unit runs on our proprietary low-latency ARM firmware, communicating natively with Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024, X-Plane 12, and Prepar3D right out of the box.

Start with a single autopilot module on your desk and expand over time into a full cockpit pedestal and overhead setup. Our modular aluminum mounting brackets lock together seamlessly as your home flight deck grows.

ABOUT US

Born in the Cockpit. Built for Your Desk.

BGS Avionics was founded by a team of aerospace engineers, CNC machinists, and lifelong flight simulation pilots who grew tired of compromise. For decades, home cockpit builders had to choose between fragile, lightweight plastic peripherals or five-figure commercial training devices. We set out to bridge that gap.

Every module that leaves our workshop is drafted from authentic flight deck schematics, milled from solid aircraft-grade aluminum, and hand-assembled with industrial-grade switches and custom backlighting. Whether you are practicing single-engine ILS approaches or managing a full transoceanic flight deck, our hardware delivers the tactile feedback, weight, and precision of a real commercial airliner.
